ApexFitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

ApexFitness is an Android fitness app made by Leo Lennards. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and how you can remove it.

What the app collects

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your sign-in method (email and password, or Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to me.
  • Fitness information you enter: your fitness level, goals, workout schedule, weight, routines, workout logs, personal records, challenges, water and cardio logs, and body weight and measurements if you choose to log them.
  • Basic usage data: the app uses Firebase Analytics, which collects anonymous information about how the app is used and about your device, such as the app version and device model.

How the information is used

Your information is used only to run the app: to save your workouts, show your stats and progress, and sync your data between sessions. I do not sell your information, I do not show ads, and I do not share your information with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Analytics). Each person's data is kept under their own account, and the database rules only let a signed-in user read and write their own data. Google processes this data on its servers under its own privacy terms.

Progress photos and exports

Progress photos you add are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to Firebase or anywhere else. They may be included in your phone's own backup if you have Android device backup turned on. Deleting your account also deletes these photos from the phone. If you export your workout history or share a workout summary, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Notifications

If you turn on workout reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone. These are local to your device and no information is sent anywhere to create them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of the data listed above. If you cannot use the app, email me at the address below and I will delete it for you.

Children

ApexFitness is not aimed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
